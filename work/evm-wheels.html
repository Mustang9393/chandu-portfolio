EVM Wheels

A mobility shopping experience redesigned around comparison, confidence, and faster purchase decisions.

Problem

Too much choice, not enough confidence.

Vehicle shopping is a high-intent journey with high anxiety. Users compare specs, availability, price, finance options, ownership costs, and trust signals before they ever reach checkout.

The product needed to make dense vehicle information scannable while keeping purchase paths obvious. The main design challenge was reducing decision load without hiding important detail.

The interface had to feel less like a catalogue and more like a guided buying system.

Design moves

Three systems shaped the experience.

01

Comparison first

Specs, price, and availability were grouped so users could make tradeoffs without bouncing between pages.

02

Trust on the surface

Delivery, service, warranty, and support signals were moved closer to product decisions.

03

Checkout clarity

The purchase path was broken into clear commitments: choose, configure, finance, confirm.
