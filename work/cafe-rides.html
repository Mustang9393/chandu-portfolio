Problem

Riding communities are built in fragments.

Riders discover routes in one place, cafes in another, events in group chats, and community through word of mouth. The experience is social, but the tools are scattered.

Cafe Rides brings those pieces together: a way to discover good roads, trusted stops, planned rides, and the small rituals that make a ride memorable.

The product is not about transport. It is about belonging with a route attached.

Design moves

A lifestyle product with practical bones.

01

Route plus ritual

Routes are paired with cafe stops, ride difficulty, distance, timing, and community context.

02

Events feel alive

Rides are treated like moments to join, not static listings to browse.

03

Brand-led discovery

The visual system makes the experience feel editorial, local, and premium without becoming decorative.
