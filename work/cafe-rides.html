Cafe Rides

A bike rental experience designed to make reliable rides easier to discover, compare, book, and begin without pricing surprises or operational confusion.

Role
Lead UX Designer
Duration
6 months
Responsibilities
Research, market scan, wireframes, prototypes, interviews, testing, visual design, UX writing
Project type
Bike rental website

Challenge

Bike rental needed to feel easy before the ride began.

Cafe Rides set out to create a seamless bike rental platform for people who wanted quick access to reliable, affordable rides. The existing experience around rental discovery was fragmented: options were limited, pricing was unclear, and booking often required too many manual steps.

The product goal was direct: make bike rental discovery and booking feel simple, transparent, and ready for real-world use. The brand could feel adventurous, but the interaction model had to be practical and predictable.

A rental product earns trust when users know the vehicle, the price, and the next step.

Product direction

Three questions shaped the experience.

01

Can I find the right ride?

Discovery needed clear vehicle categories, availability, rental duration, and practical comparison details.

02

Do I understand the price?

Pricing had to be legible early, including rate, deposit, add-ons, and conditions that affect the final amount.

03

Can I book without friction?

The flow needed to reduce back-and-forth by making requirements, pickup, and confirmation visible before checkout.

Solution

A cleaner rental path from search to ride.

Discover

Vehicle-first browsing

Users can scan available bikes by type, price, and booking context instead of starting with a generic catalogue.

Compare

Rental clarity

Core decision details are grouped together: availability, rental cost, pickup location, and terms.

Book

Quick confirmation

The booking path prioritizes confirmation confidence and reduces the need for manual follow-up.

Ride

Ready-to-go details

Pickup instructions and support cues help users feel prepared before the rental experience begins.

Visual system

Adventure in the brand, restraint in the interface.

Cafe Rides visual direction, used as the page cover and primary brand signal.

Why the interface stays quiet

Rental decisions require speed and accuracy. The product can carry energy through imagery and brand tone, while the booking surface stays focused on vehicle choice, pricing, confirmation, and support.

Outcome

A foundation strong enough to merge.

Cafe Rides eventually merged with EVM Wheels, which made the design work especially valuable as a focused exploration of the bike rental side of the larger mobility platform. The work clarified how rental discovery, transparent pricing, and practical booking flows should behave before scaling into a broader vehicle ecosystem.
