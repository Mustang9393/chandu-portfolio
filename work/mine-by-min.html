Mine by Min

A personal-care experience designed to feel private, clear, and calm for users navigating sensitive choices.

Role
UX design, visual direction, mobile flows
Focus
Privacy, onboarding, product education, inclusive language
Platform
Mobile-first product experience
Status
Case study draft - screenshots to be replaced

Problem

Personal care often feels impersonal.

Sensitive product categories are usually over-explained, under-designed, or wrapped in language that makes users feel like a problem to be fixed. Mine by Min needed the opposite posture.

The experience had to make product discovery feel private and informed, while keeping the interface warm enough for real people and structured enough for repeated use.

The design goal was simple: make privacy feel beautiful, not clinical.

Design moves

Calm interaction for sensitive decisions.

01

Private by default

Flows avoid performative disclosure and keep sensitive choices contained to the task.

02

Plain language

Labels and guidance are direct, non-judgmental, and easy to scan.

03

Mobile rhythm

Short screens, clear steps, and generous spacing keep the experience lightweight.

Outcome

A case study about trust, not decoration.

Mine by Min is framed as an inclusive UX problem: the product experience has to protect user dignity while still being useful, educational, and commercially clear.
